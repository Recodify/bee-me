Based on our discussions over the last few weeks, your main objective for Work Solutions Electrical Ltd has been to move away from small reactive domestic jobs and secure more profitable, larger-scale projects.
Key Growth Strategy
1. Target Larger Commercial & Development Work
You identified that the best opportunities are likely to come from:
Property developers
Building contractors
Commercial landlords
HMO conversions
Care homes
Office refurbishments
Fire alarm and emergency lighting projects
Full and partial rewires
EV charging installations at scale
The goal is to replace numerous small £100-£300 jobs with fewer projects worth several thousand pounds or more.
2. Reposition the Website
We discussed changing the messaging on your website so it focuses less on:
Changing light fittings
Extra sockets
Small domestic repairs
And more on:
Commercial electrical installations
Full rewires
HMO electrical packages
Fire alarm systems
Emergency lighting
Landlord compliance
Development and refurbishment projects
NICEIC Approved Contractor credentials
The website should demonstrate that Work Solutions can handle substantial projects rather than just small domestic works.
3. Build Relationships with Developers & Contractors
Rather than relying solely on Checkatrade and domestic enquiries, we discussed proactively approaching:
Local building contractors
Property developers
Letting agents
Property management companies
Architects
Quantity surveyors
The aim is to become their preferred electrical contractor for repeat work.
4. Increase Recurring Revenue
We identified opportunities for ongoing service contracts such as:
Emergency lighting testing
Fire alarm maintenance
EICR programmes
Commercial maintenance contracts
Landlord property portfolios
Planned preventative maintenance
These provide more predictable revenue than one-off domestic jobs.
5. Improve Marketing Focus
Rather than marketing "everything to everyone," the strategy was to focus messaging around:
Commercial clients
Landlords with multiple properties
Developers
Refurbishment specialists
This helps attract the type of enquiries you actually want.
6. Showcase Bigger Projects
We discussed creating case studies and highlighting:
Consumer unit upgrades
Rewires
Commercial installations
EV charger projects
Fire alarm systems
Multi-property projects
Potential clients need to see evidence that Work Solutions undertakes larger, more complex jobs.
7. Protect Profitability
A recurring theme was avoiding jobs that:
Carry high dispute risk
Generate little profit
Require excessive administration
Take up valuable engineer time
You have consistently looked at whether smaller jobs contribute meaningfully to overall business growth.
The 12-Month Plan We Effectively Outlined
Immediate (0-3 Months)
Update website positioning.
Create commercial-focused service pages.
Establish a LinkedIn presence.
Contact local builders and developers.
Publish case studies of larger jobs.
Medium Term (3-12 Months)
Secure regular contractor relationships.
Win HMO and refurbishment projects.
Build recurring maintenance contracts.
Increase average job value significantly.
Long Term
Become known locally not just as an electrician, but as a commercial electrical, fire alarm, compliance and refurbishment contractor capable of delivering complete electrical packages for developers, landlords and commercial clients.
In summary, the biggest conclusion from our discussions was:
Stop chasing more small jobs and start attracting fewer, larger, repeat clients who generate ongoing work and higher margins. This is likely to have the greatest impact on the growth and value of Work Solutions Electrical Ltd.
